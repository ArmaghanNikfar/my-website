Selected Research

A Novel LSTM-Based Multimodal System for Robust Diagnosis of Respiratory Infectious Diseases

An improved LSTM (iLSTM) model diagnosing colds, flu, and related infectious diseases from structured patient data — vitals, lab results, and symptoms — combined with OCR-extracted test results and a natural-language QA layer that gathers information directly from patients. Designed as a decision-support tool that reduces diagnostic error and speeds up triage.

Clinical Collaboration Carried out with practicing physicians. My husband, a physician in Saint Petersburg completing his surgical residency at I.I. BenchCouncil Transactions on Benchmarks, Standards and Evaluations · ScienceDirect 2025

Predicting the Number of Incoming Calls to a Call Center Using Deep Learning

A Bidirectional LSTM (BLSTM) forecasting model trained on four years of real call-center data from Shatel, an Iranian ISP. Predicts incoming call volume accounting for holidays, seasonal patterns, and cultural factors — ~90% accuracy, outperforming unidirectional-LSTM approaches, directly improving staffing efficiency.
